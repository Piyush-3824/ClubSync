What ClubSync Does

🔍

Discover Clubs

Browse 120+ verified college clubs across technical, cultural, sports, and social categories. Filter by interest, tag, or activity.

📋

Apply Instantly

Submit applications directly through the portal. Track status, get interview slots, and receive decisions — all in one place.

⭐

Save & Compare

Bookmark clubs you love. Compare requirements, timelines, and perks side-by-side before committing to an application.

🔔

Live Notifications

Never miss a recruitment drive. Get real-time alerts for deadlines, interview schedules, and application updates.

⚙️

Coordinator Tools

Club coordinators can manage drives, review applicants, schedule interviews, and track member analytics — all from one dashboard.

🤝

Build Community

Connect with like-minded peers. ClubSync isn't just about joining clubs — it's about finding your place in college life.
